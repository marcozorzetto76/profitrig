How much should you charge for mobile dog grooming in 2026?

Mobile dog grooming prices between $75 and $200 per appointment in 2026, depending on dog size. That is roughly a 30–50% premium over salon grooming, and the premium is not optional — it is what pays for the van and the driving.

The cost salon groomers do not have

A salon groomer fills the gaps between dogs. You cannot — you are driving.

Drive time is unpaid time that still burns fuel and van depreciation, and it is the most underpriced cost in mobile grooming. Add ten minutes of driving to every appointment and two things happen at once: your cost per dog rises, and the number of dogs you can do in a day falls. It hits you twice.

How to check your own number

StepWhat to work out
1Total annual costs: van, insurance, products, equipment allowance, and your own wage at a rate you would pay someone else
2Appointments you realistically complete in a year — not the theoretical maximum
3Divide. That is your break-even per appointment. Add margin to get your floor

An honest warning. If your minimum price comes out above the going rate in your area, the arithmetic is not wrong. It is telling you the cost structure needs more appointments per day, less driving, or a shift towards larger dogs and add-ons.

Mobile grooming is a thin-margin trade run on an expensive asset. Better to know in August than in January.

Three mistakes that cost the most

Flat pricing regardless of condition. A badly matted coat can double an appointment. Charge the same as a clean one and the difficult dogs are paid for by the easy ones.

No travel radius. Every mile beyond your standard area costs fuel, depreciation and a slot you could have filled.

Undercharging the premium. Mobile is a convenience service. Pricing at salon rates gives away the entire reason the business model exists.
