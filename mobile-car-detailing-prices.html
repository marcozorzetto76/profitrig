How much should you charge for mobile car detailing in 2026?

Mobile detailing prices at $75–$250 for a single interior or exterior detail, $150–$450 for a full detail, and $600–$2,500 for ceramic coating. Vehicle size adds 15–60%. Condition adds 25–100% — and condition is the one that ruins days.

The vehicle size is not what kills your day

A clean interior detail runs about 90 minutes. The same package on a car with pet hair, food odour and spilled coffee runs three to four hours.

Same job on the invoice. Triple the labour. And if you quoted it as a standard interior, you just turned a two-car day into a one-car day — which means you lost the second job as well as the margin on the first.

The fix is not complicated. Ask for photos before quoting, and put condition in the price as an explicit line rather than absorbing it. Customers accept it when it is named up front. They resent it when it appears afterwards.
