How much should you charge for pressure washing in 2026?

Most US pressure washing work prices between $0.15 and $0.40 per square foot, or $50 to $120 per hour, with a minimum job fee of $180 to $400. Driveways sit at the low end. Roofs and wood restoration sit at the high end.

That is the short answer. The rest of this page is the part that matters more: whether those numbers work for you, because a market rate you cannot deliver profitably is just a number.

Why the market rate is only half the answer

Market rate tells you what customers will pay. It does not tell you what the job costs you. Those are different numbers, and only one of them decides whether you make money.

Most operators underestimate their real operating costs by 35–40%. The gap comes from three places: unbillable hours, equipment replacement, and the true per-mile cost of the vehicle. A $250 driveway at the going rate can still lose money if your day only contains five billable hours.

Do the check before you quote

Three steps. It takes ten minutes once, then thirty seconds per job.

StepWhat to work out
1Your break-even cost per billable hour — not per worked hour
2The hours the job will really take, including travel and setup
3Whether the market price for that area covers step 1 × step 2, with margin left

If it does not, the answer is not to charge above market. The answer is that the job is not worth taking, or your cost structure needs fixing. Both are useful to know before you send the number.

Three mistakes that cost the most

No minimum service fee. Setup, travel and teardown cost the same on a 200 sq ft job as on a 2,000 sq ft one. Without a floor, small jobs quietly subsidise themselves out of your margin.

Pricing off competitors. Their cost structure is not yours. Copying a rate from a competitor with three trucks and better routing is how you end up busy and broke.

Ignoring condition. Heavy organic growth, oil staining or old sealer can triple the labour on an identical square footage. Price the condition, not just the area.
